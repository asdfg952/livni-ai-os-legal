Purpose LIVNI AI OS is a privately operated analytics platform used to review and analyze authorized social media account information, including TikTok account statistics and public video performance. AI-assisted analysis of TikTok data is not currently enabled. It may be enabled in the future only as described in the Privacy Policy, and only after the Privacy Policy has been updated. 3. TikTok Integration LIVNI AI OS uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Display API with the read-only permissions user.info.basic, user.info.stats and video.list to access account information, follower statistics and publicly available video data. Access requires authorization from the relevant TikTok account holder. LIVNI AI OS does not publish, modify, or delete TikTok content through this integration. 4. Authorized Use The service is intended for internal, authorized use only. Users must have permission to connect and analyze the TikTok accounts they authorize. 5. Data and Privacy Information obtained through TikTok is processed for analytics, reporting, and content performance evaluation. It is stored locally on the system owner's computer, with access credentials encrypted, and is kept for a limited time: statistics, video metadata and logs for up to 12 months, and copies of manual imports for up to 30 days. Data that has reached its limit is removed by the system owner using the tool's deletion function, and data can be deleted on request. Details are provided in our Privacy Policy. 6. Service Availability LIVNI AI OS is provided on an as-available basis. Features may change or become unavailable due to technical limitations or third-party API changes. 7. Termination Authorized users may discontinue use at any time and revoke TikTok access either with the "Disconnect" function in LIVNI AI OS or through their TikTok account settings.
