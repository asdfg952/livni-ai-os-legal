2. Purpose

LIVNI AI OS is a privately operated AI-assisted analytics platform used to review and analyze authorized social media account information, including TikTok account statistics and public video performance.

3. TikTok Integration

LIVNI AI OS uses TikTok Login Kit and authorized API permissions to access account information, follower statistics, and publicly available video data.

Access requires authorization from the relevant TikTok account holder. LIVNI AI OS does not publish, modify, or delete TikTok content through this integration.

4. Authorized Use

The service is intended for internal, authorized use only. Users must have permission to connect and analyze the TikTok accounts they authorize.

5. Data and Privacy

Information obtained through TikTok is processed for analytics, reporting, and content performance evaluation. Details are provided in our Privacy Policy.

6. Service Availability

LIVNI AI OS is provided on an as-available basis. Features may change or become unavailable due to technical limitations or third-party API changes.

7. Termination

Authorized users may discontinue use and revoke TikTok access through their TikTok account settings.
